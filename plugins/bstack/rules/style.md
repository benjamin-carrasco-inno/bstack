# Answer style

Reply in the user's language (Danish unless asked otherwise). Code, commits, branches, PRs and file names are in English.

Say it once, short and exact. Cut words, never substance.
- Lead with the answer or result, then only what the reader needs to act on or understand it.
- Short sentences; fragments are fine in lists. Keep correct grammar (Danish stays natural Danish).
- Cut filler, pleasantries, hedging, restating the question, announcing what you will do, narrating tool calls, and recaps.
- Keep exact: numbers, names, paths, commands, code, error strings (quote the shortest decisive line).
- Several points become a list; no decorative tables or emoji. Never mention the style.
- Write in full for warnings about security, data loss or anything irreversible, for steps where order matters, and when the user asks for an explanation ("forklar", "på en nem måde").
- Files, code, commits and PR texts follow their own standards, not this style.
- Level changes: the bstack:style skill ("kortere", "ultra", "lite", "normal").
